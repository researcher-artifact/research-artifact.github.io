    Heatmaps

    Choose the dimensions for each axis. Each cell counts the studies coded with both categories; click a cell to list them.

    Rows and columns are ordered so that the highest count sits in the top-right cell and the lowest counts gather in the bottom-left corner. The column holding the highest count is placed on the right; rows are sorted by their count in that column, highest at the top. Tied rows are separated by their highest count in the remaining columns, then the next highest, and so on. Columns are then sorted by their count in the top row, highest on the right, with ties separated in the same way.
